Imagine your skin as a silent worker, clocking in every day, protecting you from everything the outside world throws at you from UV rays, pollution, blue light, and temperature shifts. Most of the time, we don’t think about it. However, these daily environmental stressors can leave lasting marks on your skin, literally.
