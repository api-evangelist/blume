Fading dark spots and acne scars is a journey, not a sprint. Dark spots, whether left behind by past breakouts, prolonged sun exposure, or hormonal fluctuations, don’t disappear overnight. The truth is, the speed and effectiveness of fading your hyperpigmentation depend not only on the products you use but also on the daily choices you make for your skin and overall health.
