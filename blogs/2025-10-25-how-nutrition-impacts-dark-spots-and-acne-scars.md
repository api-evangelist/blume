We talk a lot about serums, spot treatments, and routines (because, yes, they do work), but there’s something that often gets overlooked: the influence nutrition can have on skin healing. Just like stress, sleep, and sun exposure, nutrition is a lifestyle factor that affects how your skin looks and feels. What we eat influences everything from inflammation to collagen production, two key factors in whether scars fade quickly or stick around.
