When it comes to fading dark spots, one-size-fits-all formulas rarely work, especially for sensitive or melanin-rich skin. The harsh brighteners that promise “instant results” often rely on high concentrations of acids or strong bleaching agents that can disrupt your barrier and cause irritation, dryness, or even rebound hyperpigmentation (when spots return darker than before). For many people, that cycle of over-treating and reacting becomes exhausting and counterproductive.
