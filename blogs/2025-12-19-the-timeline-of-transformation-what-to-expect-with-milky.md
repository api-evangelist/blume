You’ve probably noticed two things very quickly in your search to fade dark spots. There’s no shortage of advice out there and almost all of it promises results faster than skin biology realistically allows. Dark spots, acne marks, and scars don’t disappear overnight and when brands suggest otherwise, they often set up feelings of frustration, self blame, and skincare burnout.
