If you’ve ever searched for the best treatment for dark spots on the face, you know the struggle: endless options, confusing ingredient lists, and price tags that make you hesitate. Even if you splurge on a $90+ serum, dermatologists remind us that fading hyperpigmentation takes months of consistent use. That means if a product is too expensive, most of us won’t stick with it long enough to see results.
