Sleep isn’t just a time for rest, it’s also one of the most critical moments for your skin’s repair and regeneration. While skincare routines, serums, and creams play an important role in fading dark spots and healing acne scars, the quality of your sleep can dramatically affect how your skin responds to these products. In this post, we’ll explore the science behind sleep and skin repair, how sleep influences dark spots, acne scar healing, and overall skin health, and practical steps you can take to maximize your skin’s nighttime recovery.
