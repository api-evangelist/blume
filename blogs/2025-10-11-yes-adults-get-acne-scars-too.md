When most people think of acne, images of teenage skin come to mind: the high school breakout, the awkward phase, the flare-ups everyone “grows out of.” But the truth is, acne doesn’t disappear when you leave your teen years behind. Adults get acne too and with it comes the very real possibility of adult acne scars. Adult acne scars are more common than many realize, and yet they are often stigmatized.
