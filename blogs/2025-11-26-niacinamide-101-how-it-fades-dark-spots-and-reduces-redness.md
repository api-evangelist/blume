If you’ve been exploring ways to fade dark spots, calm redness, and improve your overall skin texture, chances are you’ve heard about niacinamide . This powerhouse ingredient has taken the skincare world by storm and for good reason. Praised as a multitasking hero, niacinamide is hyped not just for its ability to reduce hyperpigmentation, but also for its role in calming irritation, strengthening the skin’s natural barrier, and supporting overall skin health.
