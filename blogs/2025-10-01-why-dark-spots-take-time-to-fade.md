In our quest to fade dark spots on skin, it's easy to get discouraged when progress seems slow. Whether you're dealing with lingering acne marks, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH), or sun-induced discoloration, the journey to clear, even-toned skin is rarely quick. In this post, we’ll explore how long dark spots take to fade, pinpoint common missteps that slow the process, and introduce Blume’s Milky Fade Spot & Scar Serum as a consistent, skin-kind solution.
